# EbolaVCR: Ebola Virus Component and Resistance Database

**EbolaVCR** is a specialized, manually curated database designed to provide a comprehensive resource on the Ebola virus (EBOV).
It integrates detailed information regarding viral components, experimental drugs, and genomic variations to support the global research community in developing effective therapeutics and vaccines.

**Web Server:** https://webs.iiitd.edu.in/oscadd/ebola/

This can also be found on Zenodo at https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.20068108

## About the Database

The Ebola virus causes a severe and often fatal hemorrhagic fever in humans and non-human primates.
To assist researchers in managing the vast and scattered data related to EBOV, EbolaVCR serves as a centralized platform that catalogs viral proteins, potential inhibitors, and the impact of mutations on drug efficacy.

The database integrates data from several high-quality resources:
* **Genomic Information:** Sourced from NCBI, UniProt, and PDB.
* **Experimental Drugs:** Compiled from published literature and clinical trial reports.
* **Mutational Data:** Extracted from various outbreaks and experimental studies.


## Key Features

### 1. Viral Components and Architecture
* Provides detailed information on the **structural proteins** of EBOV: NP, VP35, VP40, GP, sGP, VP30, VP24, and L.
* Includes sequence data, functional annotations, and available 3D structures for these proteins.
* Catalogs information on the viral genome, including non-coding regions and regulatory elements.



### 2. Experimental Therapeutics
* Maintains a repository of experimental drugs and vaccines tested against EBOV.
* Includes information on small molecules, siRNA, antibodies, and viral-vector-based vaccines.
* Provides experimental details such as inhibitory concentrations and the stage of clinical or preclinical development.

### 3. Resistance and Mutational Analysis
* Catalogs thousands of natural and experimental mutations identified in EBOV isolates.
* Highlights "Resistance-Associated Mutations" (RAMs) that may hinder the effectiveness of existing or candidate drugs.
* Provides tools to map these mutations onto the viral protein structures to predict their functional impact.


## Integrated Tools and Analysis

EbolaVCR offers several user-friendly modules to facilitate research and data extraction:
* **Search & Browse:** Users can query by protein name, drug name, or specific mutation.
* **Similarity Search:** Allows users to find molecules similar to known EBOV inhibitors based on chemical structures.
* **Mutation Mapping:** A specialized tool to visualize where specific mutations occur within the 3D structure of viral proteins.
* **Alignment Tool:** Enables users to align query sequences with wild-type or mutant EBOV sequences to identify variations.


## Applications

* **Drug Discovery:** Identifying conserved regions in the viral genome as potential targets for new antivirals.
* **Outbreak Monitoring:** Tracking how the virus evolves during an epidemic to assess if diagnostic tools or treatments remain effective.
* **Vaccine Development:** Utilizing structural data to design immunogens that elicit a broad and protective immune response.
